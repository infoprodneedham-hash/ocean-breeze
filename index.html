Welcome to the Coastal Sunroom

A tranquil sanctuary for tea, gardens, and ocean breezes

Step into a space where the warmth of sunlit glass meets the refreshing calm of the sea. Our sunroom and garden-themed tea house offers a place to slow down, sip thoughtfully crafted blends, and enjoy the coastal flora surrounding us.

About the Author

Host & Curator

Hello and welcome! I created this coastal sunroom tea space as an homage to quiet ocean mornings and vibrant seaside gardens. My passion is crafting floral teas, sharing stories of coastal living, and providing a peaceful haven for every visitor.
